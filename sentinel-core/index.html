Sentinel Core — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026
Publisher: Yusuf KEF
Contact: kefyusuf@gmail.com

Scope and local game data

This policy covers the Android release of Sentinel Core. Scores, sector progress, stars, virtual credits, unlocked and equipped skins, language, sound preferences and gameplay settings are stored on your device to provide gameplay and retain progress. The publisher does not operate a separate gameplay server. You can play locally without connecting a Play Games profile.

Google Play Games and cloud saves

On Android, the Google Play Games SDK is initialized and may attempt automatic authentication using your Google account. When Play Games is connected, the app can access your Play Games player identifier, display name, title and profile image URLs. It submits scores and achievement progress to Google and stores a cloud snapshot containing scores, sector unlocks and stars, virtual credits, inventory and equipped items, interface language and a synchronization timestamp. The app can also read Google-provided player statistics, such as session count, session length and purchase statistics; access to these statistics does not mean that this release offers real-money purchases. These services support achievements, leaderboards and restoring progress. Google processes service, account, device and diagnostic information under its own policies. Continuing offline skips the app's pending sign-in or restore flow; it does not delete an existing Google profile or cloud save.

Advertising, payments, permissions and diagnostics

This release does not include advertising, real-money in-app purchases or a publisher-operated analytics or remote crash-reporting service. Shop credits are earned in the game. The game does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone. It uses the network for Google Play Games, local storage for progress, and device services for audio and vibration. Fonts and this policy are bundled with the game. The publisher does not sell game data. Google and Android services may process their own diagnostics independently.

Service providers and security

Google operates Play Games authentication, achievements, leaderboards and cloud storage. Google Play distribution and Android backup or restoration are governed by Google's policies and your device/account settings. The publisher does not receive your Google password. Network game-service requests use the Google SDK. Local records remain in the app's storage area; device security and backups also affect their protection. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deleting data

Local progress remains until you clear app data or uninstall the game. Use Android Settings > Apps > Sentinel Core > Storage > Clear data to reset local progress and inventory. This action does not by itself remove Google Play Games cloud data, and device backups or a connected profile may restore progress. Manage or delete game data using Google Play Games settings, including the Delete Play Games account and data controls, and manage Android backups separately. The publisher does not operate your Google account and cannot directly delete Google's account records. Contact kefyusuf@gmail.com if you need help finding the appropriate deletion controls.

Support correspondence

If you voluntarily email kefyusuf@gmail.com, your email address, message and attachments are used to answer your request. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. The email provider processes email under its own policies.

Policy website, children and changes

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses under its privacy policies; the policy page has no advertising or analytics scripts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the publisher, contact the address above. Changes to the game's online services, advertising, payments or data handling will require updated policy and store disclosures before release. The date above identifies this version of the policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or support, contact Yusuf KEF at kefyusuf@gmail.com.
